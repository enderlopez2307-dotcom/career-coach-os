# Opportunity Scoring Rubric — TEMPLATE

> Copy to `config/scoring_rubric.md`. Every role Scout finds gets scored
> 1–10 against this before it's written to the vault. `/onboard` can draft
> this from your profile; edit the anchors freely, they're what make the
> score mean something specific to you rather than a generic guess.

## Dimensions & Weights
| Dimension | Weight | What it measures |
|---|---|---|
| Role fit | 25% | How closely the title and scope match your target roles |
| Company fit | 20% | The kind of employer you actually want |
| Skill/domain fit | 20% | Uses your real strengths, not just adjacent ones |
| Comp alignment | 15% | Meets or beats your floor (unknown = mid score, flagged) |
| Location fit | 15% | Matches your location rules |
| Growth signal | 5% | Hiring, funded, building, not contracting |

## Scoring Guide
For each dimension, write what a 10, a 6, and a 2 look like *for you*.
Concrete anchors, not vibes — that's what makes two roles scored a week
apart comparable.

## Formula
```
score = Σ (dimension_score × dimension_weight)
```

## The one rule no weight can override
A role's final score is capped at 4 if the live posting states a hard
requirement — years of experience, seniority band, a required certification
— the candidate clearly doesn't meet. Scout checks this against
`profile.md` only after actually reading the posting, never from the title.
No combination of weights above rescues a role past that cap; a great title
match doesn't make an unmet hard requirement disappear.

## Action Thresholds
| Score | Action |
|---|---|
| 8–10 | High priority — daily digest, run `/warm-path` |
| 6–7 | Watch — logged, weekly review |
| 4–5 | Low signal — logged only |
| < 4 | Discard |
