description: "Before applying anywhere: who do you actually know, or can reach, there? Returns a verdict and, if one exists, the play."
---

**Agent:** `coach` · runs in this session.
**Usage:** `/warm-path <Company>`

## Task
1. **Check the opportunity note's `Verified` field first.** If it says
   `not yet`, or the date is more than 3 days old, say so before anything
   else and offer to re-run Scout's verification on this one role.
   Reaching toward a role that's gone stale wastes the contact's time and
   the candidate's one shot at that door.
2. Check `vault/Tracking/Contacts.md` for anyone already there.
3. Check the company's opportunity note for a named hiring manager (from
   Scout's human-path stub).
4. If nothing's on file, run one round of `WebSearch` for "<Company> head
   of <function>" and for the candidate's own network overlap (former
   colleagues, alumni, mutuals) if that's realistic to search for.
5. Verdict:
   - **Warm** — name the person, the relationship, and the ask.
   - **Reachable but cold** — name the person and why they're worth a cold,
     well-researched note anyway.
   - **None found** — say so plainly. Applying cold is still allowed, but
     log it as `cold — no human attached` on the opportunity note.
6. If warm or reachable, offer to draft — on a yes, hand off to
   `/draft-message` or `/network-outreach`, and remind them the application
   itself should follow 48-72 hours after outreach, not alongside it.
