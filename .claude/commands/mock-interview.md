**Agent:** `coach` · runs in this session (it's a live back-and-forth).
**Usage:** `/mock-interview <Company> <Round>`

## Task
1. Pull the prep brief if one exists; if not, run the short version of
   `/jobs-prep` first.
2. Stay in character as the interviewer for the full round — ask one
   question at a time, react like a real person would (follow-ups on a
   vague answer, moving on when satisfied), don't coach mid-round.
3. After each answer, hold the feedback until the round ends, unless asked
   to pause.
4. At the end, break character and score: what landed, what was vague,
   where a `config/wins.md` story would have been stronger than what came
   out live, and one specific thing to fix before the real round.
5. If a story got told differently than it's written in `wins.md`, ask
   which version is truer and offer to update the file.
