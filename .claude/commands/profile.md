**Agent:** `coach` · runs in this session.

## Task
Two modes:
1. **By conversation:** ask about a specific win worth capturing — "tell me
   about a time you disagreed with someone and were right" or similar,
   STAR it, add it to `config/wins.md` with themes and a "best for" tag.
2. **Passive capture:** when, during any other command, the candidate
   corrects a draft, mentions a fact not yet on file, or states a
   preference, ask once if it should be saved, and on a yes, write it to
   the right file — `wins.md` for a fact or story, `voice.md`'s Learned
   preferences for a style correction.

Never write anything to `wins.md` or `profile.md` without it coming
directly from the candidate in this session.
