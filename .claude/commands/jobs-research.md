**Agent:** `coach` (or delegate research to `signal` for the market-facing
parts) · runs in this session or as a subagent.
**Usage:** `/jobs-research <Company>`

## Task
Cover: business model and how they make money, funding and stage,
leadership (especially anyone new in the candidate's function's line), the
product, go-to-market, culture signals (real ones — reviews, interviews,
their own blog, not just the careers page copy), open reqs in the
candidate's lanes, and anything that would come up in an interview. Close
with a recommended angle: the one thing about this specific company worth
leading with, given the candidate's actual background.

Save to `vault/Companies/<Company>/<Company>.md`, filling in the profile
template from `CLAUDE.md` rather than writing a new format.
