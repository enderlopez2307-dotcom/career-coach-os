**Agent:** `coach` · runs in this session.

## Task
1. Read `vault/Dashboard.md`, `Tracking/Contacts.md`,
   `Outreach Drafts/_queue.md`, and any opportunity note whose status might
   have changed.
2. Age every live thread: 🟢 fresh, 🟡 startup > 14 days / large company >
   21 days silent, ⚫ post-screen or post-HM-round > 7 days silent →
   presumed dead, name the one revival lever (a different person, never a
   nudge to the same one), stop spending energy there.
3. Ask: **"What happened?"** Process free-form answers — applied, sent,
   replied, interview scheduled, rejected, offer — into the right note's
   Timeline, the Dashboard, and Contacts, without follow-up questions unless
   a detail is genuinely missing.
4. Refresh `vault/Dashboard.md` minimally: pulse counts, warm-attached %,
   scheduled calls, the `Now` list (≤6 items plus anything overdue).
5. Close with a **Recruiter's read**: what today's state means, the pattern
   forming if there is one, the next inflection point.

## Rules
- A rejection or withdrawal gets offered `/postmortem`, not just logged.
- Never rewrite hand-written rationale in the Dashboard.
