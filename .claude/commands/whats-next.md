description: "Choose-your-own-adventure session. Full pipeline sweep, then the 3-5 highest-leverage moves toward landing the next role. Pick one, do it together, the vault syncs, the menu re-ranks. Triggers on 'what should we do next?'"
---

**Agent:** `coach` · runs in this session (it's a loop with the candidate).

## Task
### Step 1 — Sweep
`config/profile.md`, `config/scoring_rubric.md`, `vault/Dashboard.md`,
`vault/Strategy.md`, `vault/Outreach Drafts/_queue.md`, `Tracking/Contacts.md`,
the newest daily scan and market pulse, and every opportunity note that's
live, overdue, or a menu candidate.

### Step 2 — Three buckets, 10-15 candidates before ranking
**A. Advance existing:** prep for scheduled calls, referral asks, replies
to people who wrote back, entries in the queue awaiting review. Never
nudges or thank-yous: the candidate messages each person once and doesn't send
post-interview follow-ups.
**B. Open new:** unactioned high-scoring roles that are `Verified`
(anything whose `Verified` date is past 3 days old gets Scout's
verification re-run before it's treated as open), fresh ATS checks on
Tier 1, queue refill if it's thin.
**C. Grow connections:** contacts due a touch, warm-ups, peer outreach at
companies with a live role, dormant-relationship revivals.

### Step 3 — Rank by impact × urgency × probability
Interviews inside 72h and closing windows go first. A bucket untouched in
~7 days gets its best action promoted. Hard filters from the profile always
apply. Seat over company.

### Step 4 — The menu
```
**What's next — <Day, Mon DD>**
*Pulse: N live threads · N in queue · N threads gone quiet · last outreach N days ago.*
**1. <Action>.** <2-4 sentences: history, why now, closing window.> *What we'd do:* <deliverable>. *~time.*
**2.** …
Reply 1-N, "more", or tell me something I don't know.
```
Every option names real companies, people, dates. No generic advice.

### Step 5 — Execute
If the action is an application, run `/warm-path` first. Drafts go through
the queue per `CLAUDE.md`, never anywhere else. Route to the matching
command (`/jobs-prep`, `/mock-interview`, `/jobs-cover`, `/jobs-research`,
`/network-outreach`, `/draft-message`, `/postmortem`) and say so.

### Step 6 — Sync
Opportunity note, Dashboard, queue, Contacts, one row in
`vault/Automation/Run Log.md`.

### Step 7 — Loop
Remove the completed action, re-rank, add anything new. On stop: a
Recruiter's read on what got done, whether the pace holds, what's on top
next time.
