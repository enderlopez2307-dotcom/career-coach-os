description: A full interview prep brief for one company, role, and stage — company context, the round's format, every person in the room, and the stories most likely to land.
---

**Agent:** `coach` · runs in this session.
**Usage:** `/jobs-prep <Company> <Role> <Stage>`

## Task
1. Pull the company's research note (run `/jobs-research` first if it
   doesn't exist) and the opportunity note.
2. Research the specific interviewers if named (LinkedIn-style background
   via `WebSearch`, never scraping LinkedIn directly).
3. From `config/wins.md`, pick the 3-4 stories best matched to this stage
   and this company's likely questions — name why each was chosen.
4. Write likely questions for this stage and function, with a one-line
   angle for each, not a full scripted answer — the candidate should sound
   like themselves, not like they memorized this document.
5. List 3-5 sharp questions for the candidate to ask, specific to what
   research turned up.
6. Save to `vault/Companies/<Company>/<Company> — <Role> — Prep.md`.

Offer `/mock-interview` next.
